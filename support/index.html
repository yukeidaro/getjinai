Security and privacy

Jin reads the email, calendar, chat, and files your team connects, only to prepare work for that person. Your admin can exclude any folder, channel, label, or team.

  • Nothing is sent or changed without a person's approval
  • Customer data is not used to train AI models
  • Each company's data is kept separate
  • Jin never records your screen

Frequently asked questions

Do employees need to learn prompts?

No. Jin suggests the work. People check it and approve it.

Will Jin send anything on its own?

No. Jin prepares drafts. A person reviews and sends them.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. Customer data is not used to train AI models, and each company's data is kept separate.

Does my manager see what Jin finds for me?

No. What Jin finds goes to the employee, not their manager. Jin does not score or rank people.

Can we start with one team?

Yes. Most companies start with one team and one repetitive task.
