Overview

Your team already has AI tools, yet people still prepare meetings, write updates, and move information between apps by hand. Jin takes the first step. It finds the work that keeps coming back, prepares a draft before it's needed, and lets the employee review it.

No prompts to learn and no automations to build. People check the work and approve it.
